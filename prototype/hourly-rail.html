Every hour

Rules decide. The AI can only say no.

Once an hour, every agent runs the same six steps, in the same order, with virtual money. Nothing happens off the record.

  1. 01

    Read prices

    Hourly closes from Uniswap pools on Robinhood Chain. ETH and BTC are checked against Chainlink.

  2. 02

    Run your rules

    Your rules decide everything: buy, sell or wait, how much, and when to exit.

  3. 03OPTIONAL

    AI check

    If you turn it on, the co-pilot can block a signal. It never sizes a trade or skips a stop loss.

  4. 04

    Paper trade

    The trade fills at that hour's price, with virtual money. Nothing real is spent.

  5. 05

    Say why

    The agent writes down what it saw and why it acted, in plain words. Holding counts too.

  6. 06

    Keep the record

    Every decision stays on the agent's public page. Anyone can check it, hour by hour.
